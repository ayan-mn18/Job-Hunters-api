# LinkedIn

Used for one thing: reaching a person at a company the applicant has applied
to, or is about to. Finding who is worth asking, and sending them a short note.

It is the highest-risk surface in the product. Everything else here can fail and
cost a run; LinkedIn can fail and cost the user their account.

## Pacing

Every action is slower than it needs to be, on purpose. A page opened and acted
on within 300 ms is a machine, and the caps elsewhere are undone by acting
instantly once a page is open. Read for two to five seconds before doing
anything. Never open two pages at once. Search results are read two pages deep
at most and capped at 25 people per pass.

## Checkpoints

If a page mentions a checkpoint, a challenge, a security verification, unusual
activity, or asks to verify identity — **stop the entire run immediately**. Do
not retry more carefully, do not go back and try a different profile, do not
wait and continue. The circuit breaker trips and a person looks at it.

## Who to contact

First-degree connections first, always. They need no invitation, they answer
far more often, and reaching them takes no risk at all. Only widen to a cold
search when the first pass came up short — plenty of people already know more
useful people at a company than they remember.

Prefer someone in the same function and one level above the applicant. A
recruiter is a different kind of ask and is ranked separately.

## The note

- An invitation note is capped by LinkedIn itself. Say who you are and what you
  are asking for, in two sentences.
- A message to an existing connection can be longer, but not much.
- Ask for one specific thing: a referral for a named role, or five minutes.
- Never claim a shared school, employer or city that is not in the facts. A
  search result card does not publish education or history, so if it is not in
  the facts it is not known, and inventing it is worse than a duller note.
- One person per company per week. This is a real person's inbox.

## Do not

- Do not send anything that was not composed and reviewed upstream. Paste the
  prepared text verbatim.
- Do not follow up more than the sequence already schedules.
- Do not visit anything outside `linkedin.com`.
- Do not accept, decline, or otherwise act on invitations, posts, or messages
  that were not part of this task.
